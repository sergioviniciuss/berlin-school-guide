Do not design tag/badge visuals in this phase.

## Content Extension Contract (FRAME-02)

This is the prescriptive structural contract for the actual methodology copy work. Final sentence-level prose is authored during phase execution against `docs/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md` voice rules (parent-centered, no ranking language, hedge-preserving per Pitfall 1/8) — this section fixes **where, what heading text, what pattern, and what voice constraints**, so the executor has no structural ambiguity.

### Section order in `methodology.mdx`

Insert **two new `##` sections** between the existing "Cobertura da pesquisa" section and "Termos em alemão" (keep German-terms glossary last, as it is today):

1. `## Hierarquia das fontes`
2. `## Características da escola (tags)`

Then append **one closing version-marker line** at the very end of the file, after "Termos em alemão".

### 1. `## Hierarquia das fontes`

- Mirrors the extended `docs/RESEARCH.md` source hierarchy (official → journalism → triangulated community) in family-facing language.
- Follow the exact bullet-definition pattern already used in "Status de evidência" (`- **Termo** — explicação.`), one bullet per tier.
- Must state plainly, in PT-BR, that community-sourced information only appears when confirmed by **two independent sources** (mirrors D-08–D-14) — this is the family-facing consequence of the independence rule, not the internal rule itself.
- Example first bullet (pattern to match, not final copy): `- **Fontes oficiais** — dados do Senado de Berlim, distrito ou inspeção escolar oficial.`

### 2. `## Características da escola (tags)`

- Must explain the facts-vs-tags distinction verbatim in spirit: *fatos respondem "o que a escola tem?"; características (tags) respondem "que tipo de escola é essa?"* (per D-07/CONTEXT.md philosophy — this sentence is the product differentiator and should not be diluted).
- Must include a **confidence label** sub-explanation: confidence means evidence strength, never school quality (mirrors the existing coverage-percentage disclaimer pattern: *"Essa porcentagem indica completude da nossa pesquisa, não qualidade da escola."* — the new sentence must use the same direct, bolded-consequence structure).
- Must include a closing bullet or sentence stating **absence of a tag ≠ negative** (mirrors the existing "Missing Information" framing already used for facts in `docs/RESEARCH.md` §Missing Information and this MDX's own evidence-status bullets) — e.g. structurally: *"A ausência de uma característica significa que não encontramos evidência suficiente — não é uma avaliação negativa da escola."*
- Voice constraint (Pitfall 1): this section must not itself use banned reputation words ("procurada," "renomada," "reputação," "bem vista," "recomendada," etc.) even descriptively — it is explaining the *rule against* such language, so it must model the rule.
- `GlossaryTerm` (`src/features/guides/GlossaryTerm`) **may optionally** be used for a single standalone term worth its own definition block (e.g. "fonte triangulada") if the executor judges plain bullets insufficient — not required; the existing bullet-list style already handles comparable density successfully and is the default. `BerlinCallout` is **not** a fit for this content (it is Berlin-vs-Germany framing, not evidence framing) — do not use it here.

### 3. Version marker (closing line)

- Placement: end of file, after the German-terms section, following the exact precedent already established in `first-steps.mdx` (horizontal rule `---` + a bold lead-in phrase starting a plain paragraph — see `first-steps.mdx` lines 52–54).
- Pattern: `---` on its own line, then a paragraph starting `**Versão da metodologia:**` followed by a version number and date.
- Example (pattern to match, not final copy): `**Versão da metodologia:** v1.1 — atualizada em 26 de julho de 2026.`
- Do **not** introduce a badge, chip, or colored component for this — it renders through the existing shared `p`/`strong` MDX mapping, identical in style to every other paragraph on the page. This is a deliberate reuse of an existing pattern, not a new "muted caption" style — no new component or CSS class is needed or permitted for it in this phase.
- The authoritative version number/date itself must also be recorded in the corresponding new `docs/DECISIONS.md` entry (per Research Pattern 4) — the MDX line is the public mirror, not the source of truth.

### Route shell (`src/app/methodology/page.tsx`)

- `metadata.description` may get a small one-line wording update if tags become a headline topic of the page (e.g. mention "características" alongside "evidências, dados ausentes e cobertura da pesquisa") — this is a one-line copy edit, not a redesign. Do not change the `GuideIntro` `eyebrow`/`title` visual treatment.
- No new props, no new component usage in `page.tsx`.

### Test coverage

- Extend the existing `describe("MethodologyPage")` block in `src/app/methodology/page.test.tsx` with new assertions once heading text is finalized (e.g. `getByRole("heading", { name: /hierarquia das fontes/i })`). Keep the existing MDX mock pattern — do not switch to rendering the real MDX in this test.
