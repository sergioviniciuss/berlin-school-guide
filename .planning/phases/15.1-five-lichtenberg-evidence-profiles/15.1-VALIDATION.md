- **After every task commit:** `corepack pnpm validate:data` and the quick Jest command above
- **After every plan wave:** `corepack pnpm test`
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** `corepack pnpm test` and `corepack pnpm e2e` must be green. `e2e:ci` stays the release-shaped Playwright config; `e2e/smoke.spec.ts` is the spec this phase changes
