# Phase 15.1: Five Lichtenberg Evidence-Backed Profiles - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-23
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Add five Lichtenberg schools as full evidence-backed profiles on the Phase 13–15 model already in the production UI: factual fields with evidence, Fontes, tags where evidence earns them, and a short PT-BR Perfil da escola on every school. No schema redesign, no new tag IDs, no directory-card or compare tag surfaces, and no edits to the existing ten school payloads.

**Schools (locked):**

1. Grundschule am Neuen Tor
2. Robinson-Schule
3. Schule auf dem Lichtenberg
4. Schlaufuchs-Schule
5. Evangelische Schule Lichtenberg

Success anchors:

1. All five appear in the static directory and have `/schools/[slug]/` profiles.
2. Each school has field-level evidence and Fontes citations for authored factual fields, at the same important-field bar as the current ten, plus the website follow-up used by the three detailed schools.
3. Each school ships a short Perfil da escola, including when it has zero tags. Tags are applied only when gathered evidence earns them.
4. The existing ten records, including the Phase 14 pilots, stay unchanged. Directory counts and tests that assume 10 schools update because five records were added.
5. User-facing qualitative copy is Brazilian Portuguese.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Factual completeness
- **D-01:** Research every new school to the same important-field bar as the current ten. From the official Berlin school portrait, fill name, number, type, public/private, address, website, languages, Ganztag, and other offers the portrait lists. Anything not found stays visibly missing.
- **D-02:** After the portrait, follow the school website the way Adam-Ries-Schule, Lew-Tolstoi-Schule, and Richard-Wagner-Schule already do: welcome classes, Ganztag detail, language programs, and inspection when the site has a page for them. If neither portrait nor website covers a field, it stays missing unless D-03 fills it.
- **D-03:** When portrait and website leave an important field empty, search journalism before leaving it missing. Journalism is factual evidence only under the Phase 13 allowlist. Do not hunt journalism for fields the official sources already state.
- **D-04:** On these five, fill after-school care, Perfil oficial (`schoolProfile`), and pedagogy only when a portrait or school website states them directly. Do not copy Ganztag into after-school care, and do not copy listed offers into Perfil oficial or pedagogy. The current ten keep their existing inferred values.

### Qualitative floor
- **D-05:** Every one of the five ships a short honest Perfil da escola, even with zero tags. A factual-only profile is not enough for this phase.
- **D-06:** A thin Perfil names what the sources confirm and which decision-relevant points remain unconfirmed (for example after-school care, language support, or inclusion, when those were not found). “Unconfirmed” means the sources checked do not say so. It does not mean the school lacks that thing.
- **D-07:** Apply every v1 tag the evidence already gathered supports (portrait, school website, journalism found under D-03). No separate pass through the taxonomy. No tag quota and no tag cap. The Phase 12 facts-versus-tags rule still applies: a verified Ganztag offer is not automatically `all-day-model`.
- **D-08:** Each unconfirmed point in the Perfil cites the sources that were checked and did not state it. Those sources appear in Fontes.

### Community research
- **D-09:** Attempt `active-school-community` only when parent venues turn up during research already underway. Triangulate and fail closed when independence or corroboration is not met. A published tag is not required for phase completion. If no venue turns up, omit the tag and skip a withhold write-up.
- **D-10:** A venue “turns up” only incidentally: linked from the school website, cited in a journalism article already used under D-03, or otherwise met while reading those sources. Do not run a separate search of Reddit, Facebook, or parent forums.
- **D-11:** Write a withhold note only after a real attempt: incidental venues were checked and the tag was still withheld. Put a short note on the school record (`qualitativeResearchNotes`) and a fuller note under `docs/research/`. If no venue appears, write nothing.
- **D-12:** Failed community venues stay in that research note. They do not appear in Fontes or in the Perfil. A source on the public profile supports a published claim.

### Evangelische Schule Lichtenberg
- **D-13:** Use the same research bar as the other four (D-01–D-08), including a required Perfil. Classification is `private`, taken from the official portrait. The directory’s existing Pública / Privada filter must show that value. A field that genuinely does not apply to this private school is `not_applicable`. Anything that applies but was not found stays `missing`.
- **D-14:** When the portrait or website states that the school is evangelical or church-run, record that in Perfil oficial and repeat it in the Perfil da escola in Brazilian Portuguese, citing that source. Do not add a religious tag. Taxonomy v1 stays closed.
- **D-15:** If the portrait or website states fees or the admission path, the Perfil may include one cited sentence. Do not add fee or admissions fields.
- **D-16:** Grades, school type, and classification come from the official portrait. Do not copy the public-school default of grades 1–6.

### Scope of existing records
- **D-17:** Do not edit the existing ten records in `lichtenbergPrimarySchools`. Their payloads, inferred fields, and coverage stay as they are. Tests and copy that hardcode “10 escolas” must change because the directory grows to 15, not because those ten records were rewritten.

### Carried forward (do not reopen)
- Phase 12 taxonomy (10 tags, 4 categories), independence rules, fail closed, community evidence only for `active-school-community` and never as the sole basis, Perfil voice, facts-versus-tags.
- Phase 13 source types, factual allowlist, tag validators, confidence labels as evidence strength.
- Phase 14: `perfilDaEscola` on the school record, plain PT-BR, about one paragraph, no Zod max length, `qualitativeLastReviewed` whenever qualitative content exists, tags imply a Perfil, citation discipline is editorial.
- Phase 15: Perfil da escola then Características da escola after the header; collapsed tag plus “Ver evidência”; Fontes merges field, tag, and qualitative citations; no new profile UI in this phase.
- Tags stay off directory cards and compare.

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact Brazilian Portuguese wording for each Perfil and which tags, if any, each school earns, within D-05–D-08 and D-14–D-15.
- `researchStatus` / `coverageLevel` for the five, as long as they reflect a portrait-plus-website pass and do not mark missing fields as verified. `profile_ready` / `detailed` is the existing pair used by Adam-Ries, Lew-Tolstoi, and Richard-Wagner.
- Exact path and filename for a community withhold note under `docs/research/` when D-11 applies.
- How `primarySchool()` accepts a non-public classification, portrait grades, and direct-only after-school care / Perfil oficial / pedagogy for these five, without changing the current ten.
- Which individual fields are `not_applicable` on Evangelische Schule Lichtenberg, judged from the portrait using D-16 and `docs/research/SCHOOL_RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md`.
- If a community tag is actually published, the Perfil may state the characteristic without labeling the source as community, citing the same sources (Phase 14 citation rule and Phase 15 D-15).

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `primarySchool()` in `lichtenbergPrimarySchools` builds a portrait source plus `field()` / `evidence()` records. It currently hardcodes `classification: "public"` and grades 1–6, and it infers after-school care from Ganztag and Perfil oficial / pedagogy from offers. These five need direct values instead of that inference (D-04, D-16).
- Tags, `perfilDaEscola`, `qualitativeLastReviewed`, and `qualitativeResearchNotes` already exist on the school schema from Phases 13–14.
- Phase 15 UI already renders Perfil da escola, Características da escola, and merged Fontes. This phase authors content; it does not add profile UI.
- Directory filters already include Pública / Privada (`SchoolFilters`). A `private` classification is enough for Evangelische Schule Lichtenberg to show up there.
- `src/features/schools/school/fixtures.ts` has a synthetic private school with `not_applicable` inspection data. Use it as a schema example, not as the editorial pattern.

### Established Patterns
- Real schools live in one Lichtenberg module and are validated with `pnpm validate:data`.
- Citations point at `sourceId`s on the school `sources[]` array. Official portrait is the base source; school-website sources are added per page, as on the three detailed schools.
- Confidence labels mean evidence strength, not school quality.

### Integration Points
- The directory, profile routes, and compare table read the same school array. Adding five records changes counts. Jest and Playwright strings such as “10 de 10 escolas encontradas” in `src/features/schools/SchoolDirectory/index.test.tsx` and directory e2e specs need to follow the new total.
- Existing ten payloads stay byte-stable aside from whatever is required to let the factory accept overrides without changing their output.

</code_context>
