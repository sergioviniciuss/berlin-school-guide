# Editorial Guide

## Language

The site is written in Brazilian Portuguese.

Core German terms should be explained clearly in Portuguese when introduced.

Examples:

- Grundschule: escola primária alemã.
- Einzugsgebiet: área de residência normalmente vinculada a uma escola pública.
- Ganztag: modelo de escola em tempo integral ou com atividades ao longo do dia.
- Hort: cuidado ou atividades supervisionadas após o horário regular de aula.
- Open Day: dia de visita à escola.
- Willkommensklasse: turma de acolhimento para crianças recém-chegadas.
- after-school care: cuidado no contraturno. Preserve official German terms such as `Hort` and `eFöB` where appropriate.

## Tone

The tone should be practical, analytical, neutral, and approachable.

Avoid alarmist language, vague reassurance, and unsupported recommendations.

## School Descriptions

School pages may include editorial notes in Portuguese, but they must distinguish clearly between:

- verified facts;
- official claims;
- school-provided descriptions;
- missing information;
- unconfirmed information.

## Claims

Do not make factual claims without citations.

Do not imply school quality from incomplete evidence.

Do not describe a school as "good," "bad," "best," or "recommended" unless the claim is attributed and clearly contextualized.

## Parent-Centered Writing

Content should translate school attributes into practical family concerns.

Instead of only stating that a school has `Ganztag`, explain what families may need to verify:

- whether the schedule covers the days and hours the family needs;
- whether after-school care is included or separate;
- whether there are costs, registration steps, or capacity limits;
- whether activities are supervised, structured, or optional.

Instead of only stating that a school offers German-language support, help families understand what to ask:

- whether support is available for children arriving with little or no German;
- whether support happens inside the classroom or separately;
- whether there are welcome classes;
- how progress is communicated to parents.

## Open Day Questions

Each detailed school page should include practical questions families can ask during school visits.

Questions should reflect available research.

Examples:

- Como funciona o apoio em alemão para crianças que chegam sem fluência?
- O Ganztag está disponível todos os dias? Até que horário?
- Como a escola se comunica com famílias que ainda não falam alemão?
- As famílias podem conhecer as instalações esportivas, biblioteca ou salas especializadas?
- Como a escola orienta as famílias na transição após o 6º ano?
- Há famílias brasileiras, portuguesas ou internacionais na comunidade escolar?

## Limitations

The site should clearly explain that:

- admissions rules can change;
- school programs can change;
- leadership and staff can change;
- parent experiences are subjective;
- the site is not an official school placement authority.

## Citations In Prose

Citations should be visible near factual claims and repeated in a consolidated sources section where appropriate.
