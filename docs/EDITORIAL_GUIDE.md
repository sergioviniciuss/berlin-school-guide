# Editorial Guide

## Language

The site is written in Brazilian Portuguese.

Core German terms should be explained clearly in Portuguese when introduced.

Examples:

- Grundschule: escola primária alemã.
- Einzugsgebiet: área de residência normalmente vinculada a uma escola pública.
- Ganztag: modelo de escola em tempo integral ou com atividades ao longo do dia.
- Hort: cuidado ou atividades supervisionadas após o horário regular de aula.
- Open Day: dia de visita à escola.
- Willkommensklasse: turma de acolhimento para crianças recém-chegadas.
- after-school care: cuidado no contraturno. Preserve official German terms such as `Hort` and `eFöB` where appropriate.

## Tone

The tone should be practical, analytical, neutral, and approachable.

Avoid alarmist language, vague reassurance, and unsupported recommendations.

## School Descriptions

School pages may include editorial notes in Portuguese, but they must distinguish clearly between:

- verified facts;
- official claims;
- school-provided descriptions;
- missing information;
- unconfirmed information.

## Claims

Do not make factual claims without citations.

Do not imply school quality from incomplete evidence.

Do not describe a school as "good," "bad," "best," or "recommended" unless the claim is attributed and clearly contextualized.

## Tag Taxonomy Voice

Do not use reputation words that imply objective superiority without full attribution and a named, dated source: "procurada," "concorrida," "renomada," "reputação," "bem vista," "recomendada," and similar terms. Any such claim must be framed as what someone said ("pais relatam que..."), never as what is true ("a escola é...").

A claim that would still make sense with every citation deleted is not acceptable — delete the claim, or add the citation and attribution it requires.

Tags describe a sustained, decision-relevant characteristic — never an isolated project, a generic claim, or how much documentation exists about a school. A rich profile with many tags is not "better" than a sparse profile with one tag or none; the difference may only be how much evidence was found, not the quality of the school. Qualitative richness, like evidence coverage, is not a quality signal.

The `active-school-community` tag describes visible parent or community participation. It never implies that the community, or the school, is "better" than one without this tag.

## Facts Versus Tags

Facts answer "what does this school have?" Tags answer "what kind of school is this?" A tag is only appropriate when the school's character goes beyond what a factual field already states — a verified `Ganztag` value does not by itself justify the `all-day-model` tag, and a bilingual or SESB stream does not by itself justify `bilingual-program`. Tags exist to help a family decide whether a school matches their child, not to catalog every offer the school has.

## Parent-Centered Writing

Content should translate school attributes into practical family concerns.

Instead of only stating that a school has `Ganztag`, explain what families may need to verify:

- whether the schedule covers the days and hours the family needs;
- whether after-school care is included or separate;
- whether there are costs, registration steps, or capacity limits;
- whether activities are supervised, structured, or optional.

Instead of only stating that a school offers German-language support, help families understand what to ask:

- whether support is available for children arriving with little or no German;
- whether support happens inside the classroom or separately;
- whether there are welcome classes;
- how progress is communicated to parents.

## Perfil da Escola Voice

The "Perfil da escola" narrative synthesizes only evidence already cited elsewhere on the page — it must never introduce an uncited claim. It never ranks, scores, or compares schools, and never uses superlative or reputation language (see Tag Taxonomy Voice above).

A school with few tags and a short "Perfil da escola" is a complete, honest profile, not an incomplete one — sparse-but-honest is the goal, not a fallback. Instead of describing a school in comparative terms, describe what a family can verify and what remains unconfirmed, following the same pattern already used for `Ganztag` and German-language support above.

## Open Day Questions

Each detailed school page should include practical questions families can ask during school visits.

Questions should reflect available research.

Examples:

- Como funciona o apoio em alemão para crianças que chegam sem fluência?
- O Ganztag está disponível todos os dias? Até que horário?
- Como a escola se comunica com famílias que ainda não falam alemão?
- As famílias podem conhecer as instalações esportivas, biblioteca ou salas especializadas?
- Como a escola orienta as famílias na transição após o 6º ano?
- Há famílias brasileiras, portuguesas ou internacionais na comunidade escolar?

## Limitations

The site should clearly explain that:

- admissions rules can change;
- school programs can change;
- leadership and staff can change;
- parent experiences are subjective;
- the site is not an official school placement authority.

## Citations In Prose

Citations should be visible near factual claims and repeated in a consolidated sources section where appropriate. Prefer consolidated per-section evidence notes over inline superscript citations on every clause of narrative prose.

## Hedge Preservation In Translation

Quantifier and hedge words in source material (for example, "einige Eltern," "some parents") must be preserved with equivalent specificity when translated into Portuguese ("alguns pais," not "pais"). A second reviewer should re-check translated qualitative claims against the original-language source, not only the Portuguese draft.
