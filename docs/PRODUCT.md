# Product

## Purpose

Berlin School Guide helps Brazilian families already living in Berlin understand the local primary education system and evaluate nearby primary schools with transparent, evidence-backed information.

The site is written in Brazilian Portuguese and assumes readers may be unfamiliar with German educational terms, Berlin-specific school rules, and the practical steps involved in choosing and visiting schools.

## Editorial Position

The product is an evidence-first decision-support tool with the tone of a practical relocation guide.

It does not rank schools as universally "best" or assign overall school quality scores. Instead, it helps families compare schools based on verified information, clearly labeled evidence, and their own priorities.

## Primary Audience

Brazilian families living in Berlin who are evaluating primary schools for their children.

The first version prioritizes families who need to understand how the Berlin primary school system works before making a school choice.

## V1 Goals

V1 should provide immediate value while proving that the research methodology is trustworthy and scalable.

V1 includes:

- a comprehensive guide to the Berlin primary education system;
- a broad static directory of public and private primary schools in Berlin using basic official data;
- 20-30 carefully researched school profiles;
- searchable and filterable static school data;
- transparent comparison criteria;
- practical checklists for school visits, enrollment preparation, and family decision-making;
- clear citations and evidence for factual claims.

## V1 School Scope

The directory should include both public and private primary schools.

Detailed school profiles should initially prioritize public schools, while selected private, bilingual, or international schools may be included when especially relevant to international and Brazilian families.

The detailed V1 set should prioritize schools relevant to international and Brazilian families while maintaining geographic diversity across Berlin. The exact set may evolve as research progresses.

## Parent-Centered Product Principle

The website should answer real parent questions rather than simply expose school data.

Content should translate raw school attributes into practical family concerns, such as:

- whether a child will receive German-language support;
- whether the school schedule works for working parents;
- how the school communicates with families who do not speak German fluently;
- what families should verify during a school visit;
- what information is missing or not confirmed.

## Out Of Scope For V1

V1 does not include:

- user accounts;
- authentication;
- personalized dashboards;
- backend database;
- production server-side APIs;
- user-generated ratings or reviews;
- subjective "best schools" rankings;
- teacher quality scores;
- AI-generated recommendations without verifiable evidence;
- detailed research coverage for every Berlin school;
- automatic scraping of third-party review websites;
- route planning;
- live public transport integration;
- school application or enrollment management;
- multilingual support;
- mobile app development.

## Product Principles

- Explain the system before asking users to compare schools.
- Prefer verified facts over opinion.
- Make uncertainty visible.
- Distinguish official information from school-provided claims.
- Avoid universal rankings.
- Help families prepare better questions.
- Treat missing data as useful information.
- Answer practical parent questions, not only data availability questions.
- Keep the site fully static until there is a clear need for server-side infrastructure.
