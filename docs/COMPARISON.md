# Comparison

## Philosophy

The site compares schools using transparent criteria. It does not rank schools by overall quality.

There should be no universal "best school" list and no editorial score implying that one school is objectively better than another.

## Comparison Criteria

Priority criteria include:

- communication with families;
- German language support;
- transition after grade 6;
- school profile and pedagogy;
- music, sports, STEM, language, or other educational focus;
- Ganztag;
- after-school care;
- bilingual or international support;
- welcome classes;
- available languages;
- inspection availability;
- evidence coverage.

## Evidence Coverage

Evidence coverage measures research completeness, not school quality.

It should be calculated as:

```text
verified important fields / total important fields
```

Example:

```text
22 verified fields / 25 important fields = 88%
```

This means the site has verified information for 88% of the fields considered important. It does not mean the school is "88% good."

## No Quality Score

V1 should not include a school quality score.

A future compatibility score may be considered only if it is:

- configurable by the user;
- based on user-selected priorities;
- clearly explained;
- supported by cited evidence;
- presented as compatibility, not quality.

## Missing Evidence

Missing evidence should be visible in comparison views. This helps families understand where they may need to ask direct questions during school visits.

## Inspection Availability

Inspection availability may be compared as `available`, `unavailable`, or `not_confirmed`.

This only indicates whether an official inspection or quality source was found. It must not be interpreted as a school quality signal.
